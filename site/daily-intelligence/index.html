Sources and limits

The editor's pilot is a selective reading of linked material, not comprehensive independent verification. Official announcements represent the issuer's position; publisher headlines are not fact-checked. Live publisher discovery is automatic in v0.7. Automatic independent verification, source-grounded synthesis and a fully verified daily editorial briefing still require a controlled research workflow.
